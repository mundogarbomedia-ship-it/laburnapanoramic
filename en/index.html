Getting here by bus

Coming by public transport? It's easy to get right to the door:

  1. From the main bus station in Andorra la Vella, take any bus going up to La Massana: L5, L6 or L7.
  2. In La Massana, change to the M1 local bus.
  3. The M1 stops right at the hotel.
